Start with one test product in Order Limit. This guide limits the total quantity of that product, including all its variants, to 2 units in a cart.

## Before you begin

Open the app from your Shopify admin. Prepare a product that customers can buy on your storefront. If it has variants, use two variants for the test.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open quantity rules">
Open **Apps** → **Order Limit**. On the app home page, choose **All quantity rules** and open the rule creation page.
</Step>
<Step title="Configure the rule">
Name it “Test product: maximum 2”. Select **Cart quantity limit**, leave the minimum blank, and enter `2` as the maximum. Choose **Limit each applicable item individually**.

Under **Applies to**, select **Specific products** and choose the test product.
</Step>
<Step title="Save and activate">
Set the timing to **Cart interactions and checkout**. Select **Active** and click **Save and activate**.

Return to **Quantity limits**, turn on **Enable quantity rules**, and confirm **Cart and checkout validation** is enabled. Otherwise, click **Enable validation**.
</Step>
<Step title="Test on your storefront">
Add 2 units and enter checkout. This rule should allow the cart. Change the quantity to 3 and verify the restriction message.

For a product with variants, also test 1 unit of variant A plus 2 of variant B. The combined product quantity of 3 should be blocked.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Expected results

| Cart | Result for this rule |
| --- | --- |
| Test product: 1 unit | Allowed |
| Test product: 2 units | Allowed |
| Test product: 3 units | Blocked |
| Another product: 3 units, no test product | Outside this rule's scope |

“Allowed” means this rule passes. Other enabled rules still apply.

<Tip>Save an unfinished rule as **Draft**. Draft rules do not enforce restrictions. Test before applying the rule to your live promotional products.</Tip>
