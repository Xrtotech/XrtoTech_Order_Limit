Use the product table in Order Limit for independent product or variant thresholds. This example caps all variants of a product at 3 units, while variant A has a separate cap of 1.

## Before you begin

Prepare variants A and B of the same purchasable product. Review [rule priority](/en/settings/rule-priority): an overlapping individual quantity rule can relax the product cap.

## Set both levels

<Steps>
<Step title="Find the product">
Open **Apps** → **订单限购** in Shopify. Choose **按产品设置数量限制** on the app home page. Search for the product under **搜索产品**.

![Search and filter for the target product on the product quantity limits page](/images/guides/寻找到需要设置的产品.png)
</Step>
<Step title="Set the whole-product cap">
On the product row, set **限制状态** to **已开启**, leave **最小值** blank and enter `3` under **最大值**. This counts all variants of the product together.

![The product row is on with a maximum of 3](/images/guides/M05-01-产品限制数量上限配置.png)
</Step>
<Step title="Set the variant A cap">
Expand the product variants. On the variant A row, select **已开启**, leave the minimum blank and set the maximum to `1`. Click **保存** at the top and wait for saving to finish.

![Filter to the target product and expand its variant rows, where each variant can have its own limits](/images/guides/M05-02-配置产品数量上限.png)
</Step>
<Step title="Enable the feature and validation">
Return to **数量限制**, turn on **开启产品数量限制**, and choose **购物车互动时和结账时**. Confirm **数量规则开关** is enabled, open the **数量规则** switch until it shows **已开启**.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Four test cases

| Cart | Expected result | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| variant B × 3 | Allowed | Product total is 3 |
| variant A × 1 + variant B × 2 | Allowed | Total 3, variant A within 1 |
| variant A × 2 | Blocked | variant A exceeds 1 |
| variant A × 1 + variant B × 3 | Blocked | Product total exceeds 3 |

Other active rules can still block a cart that passes these settings.

Variant A × 2 exceeds the variant A cap of 1, and the storefront shows the block message:

![Variant A at 2 units, the storefront says the purchase quantity cannot exceed 1](/images/guides/M05-03-设置A变体上限条件.png)

Variant A × 1 plus variant B × 2 stays within the product cap of 3, so the cart can proceed to checkout:

![Variant A 1 unit and variant B 2 units in the cart, 3 total, can proceed to checkout](/images/guides/M05-05-校验组合变体购买两件通过.png)

## Edit or pause

Change the row values and click **保存**. To pause one row, set **限制状态** to **未开启** and save. Disabling a product row does not disable its active variant rows; check each separately.

Turning off **开启产品数量限制** pauses these product and variant settings. It is separate from **数量规则**.

## If results differ

If variant A × 2 passes, check that you edited the variant A row and saved. If the whole product exceeds 3, check product status, the feature switch and overlapping individual rules. Product and individual rule limits combine using relaxed thresholds.

For a shared allowance across products, use [combined quantity](/en/scenarios/mix-and-match). Complete the [launch checklist](/en/getting-started/launch-checklist). The bold Chinese labels above match the current app.
