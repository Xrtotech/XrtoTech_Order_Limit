## Create a price rule

<Steps>
<Step title="Open price limits">
Open **Apps** → **Order Limit**, then choose **Price limits** (**价格限制**) in the sidebar. On the **Price rules** card, click **Manage rules** (**管理规则**) and create a rule.

![Price limits home page, with 价格限制 marked in the sidebar and the 价格规则 card showing 已关闭 and 管理规则](/images/guides/M08-01-价格规则创建入口.png)
</Step>
<Step title="Enter the amounts">
Fill in **Minimum amount** (**最低金额**) or **Maximum amount** (**最高金额**). At least one is required. Enter amounts in the store's base currency shown on the page.

![Create price rule page with 100 in the minimum amount field and the CNY base-currency note](/images/guides/M08-02-价格规则金额与货币.png)
</Step>
<Step title="Choose scope and rule type">
Choose all products, specific products or collections. Select **Cart price limit** or **Use different price limits by customer tag**.
</Step>
<Step title="Save and enable the feature">
Choose the timing, select **Active**, and click **Save and activate** (**保存并启用**).

Return to **Price limits** (**价格限制**) and turn on the **价格规则** switch until it shows **已开启**.

![The 价格规则 switch shows 已开启, with the 价格规则已开启 confirmation at the top](/images/guides/M08-03-价格功能开关.png)
</Step>
</Steps>

## What counts toward the amount?

| Item | Calculation |
| --- | --- |
| Applicable merchandise | Sum of current cart line amounts |
| Products outside the scope | Excluded |
| Compare-at prices | Excluded |
| Shipping and taxes | Not added as merchandise amounts |
| Discounts | Uses the current line amount available during validation; test your actual discount combinations |

For a coffee collection minimum of 100, coffee worth 80 plus a mug worth 50 still fails if the mug is outside the collection.

<Note>A specific-product or collection rule does not require customers to buy from that range when their cart contains no matching items. Equality with a minimum or maximum satisfies that threshold.</Note>

## Markets and currencies

Enter thresholds in the shop’s base currency. At checkout in another market currency, thresholds use Shopify’s checkout conversion rate and the checkout currency’s precision.

For example, a base-currency threshold of 100 converts to 120 at a rate of 1.2. This is an example rate, not a fixed rate.

## Customer groups

Add one or more tags and choose matching or non-matching customers. Any one matching tag counts as a match. Guests whose tags are not identified fall into the non-matching group.

Every matching price rule is checked. The first-match priority used by individual quantity rules does not apply to price rules.

Try [a minimum merchandise amount](/en/scenarios/minimum-order-value) or [separate wholesale and retail thresholds](/en/scenarios/customer-pricing).

## Use a maximum or a range

For a maximum only, leave the minimum blank. For a range, fill both values with the maximum at least as large as the minimum. Equal boundaries pass. Test below minimum, at minimum, at maximum and above maximum using the [launch checklist](/en/getting-started/launch-checklist).
