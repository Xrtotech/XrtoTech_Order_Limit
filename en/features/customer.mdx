Customer restrictions control who can buy applicable products. Unlike customer-tag quantity rules, these rules determine eligibility rather than the allowed number of units.

## Choose the tag direction

| Setting | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Customers with these tags cannot purchase** | Blocklist: a match with any listed tag restricts the customer |
| **Customers without these tags cannot purchase** | Allowlist: the customer needs at least one listed tag |

For VIP-only sales, add `VIP` and choose the second option. Multiple tags use “any”, not “all”. Listing `VIP` and `Wholesale` does not require both.

## Require sign-in

Enable **Require a customer account sign-in** to make guests sign in before purchasing applicable products. This helps identify tags instead of relying on an unidentified visitor.

For sign-in only, leave the tag list empty and enable the account requirement.

<Note>All buyers of the applicable products must sign in when this requirement is enabled, even customers who are not blocked by tags.</Note>

## Create a customer rule

1. Open **Customer restriction rules** from the home page and create a rule.
2. Enter tags and click **Add tag**, or skip tags for sign-in only.
3. Select the correct tag mode and account requirement.
4. Select all products, specific products or collections under applicable items.
5. Choose the timing, select **Active**, and click **Create and activate** (**创建并启用**; **保存并启用** when editing).
6. Turn on **Enable customer restriction rules** on the customer overview page.

## Purchasing is different from browsing

These rules do not automatically hide product pages, prices or collections. They check buying eligibility and are not page-access control.

Try [member exclusives](/en/scenarios/members-only) or [blocking tagged customers](/en/scenarios/block-customers).

## Require sign-in without a tag

Leave tags empty and enable the sign-in requirement. Follow [require sign-in](/en/scenarios/login-required) for setup and identity tests.
