Weight limits total applicable variant weights multiplied by cart quantities. Rules can apply to all products, selected products or collections, with optional customer-tag conditions.

## Check product weights first

**Weight limits by product** shows products and their variant weights. Create the actual restrictions under **Weight rules**.

Verify each applicable variant’s weight in Shopify product details. Missing, zero or invalid values do not contribute valid weight and can make the calculated total lower than the real total.

## Create a weight rule

<Steps>
<Step title="Open weight limits">
Open **Apps** → **Order Limit**, then choose **Weight limits** (**重量限制**) in the sidebar. On the **Weight rules** card, click **Manage rules** (**管理规则**) and create a rule.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose the unit and enter weights">
Select **Rule weight unit** (**规则重量单位**) first, then enter **Minimum weight** (**最低重量**) or **Maximum weight** (**最高重量**). At least one is required.

![Create weight rule page with kg as the rule weight unit and 2 entered as the minimum weight](/images/guides/M09-02-重量规则参数.png)
</Step>
<Step title="Choose scope and customer condition">
Select the applicable products, then choose all customers or a customer-tag condition.
</Step>
<Step title="Save and enable the feature">
Set the timing and **Active** status, then click **Save and activate** (**保存并启用**).

Return to **Weight limits** (**重量限制**) and turn on the **重量规则** switch until it shows **已开启**.

![The 重量规则 switch shows 已开启](/images/guides/M09-03-重量功能开关.png)
</Step>
</Steps>

## Calculation example

Total weight is each applicable variant’s unit weight multiplied by quantity, then summed.

| Product | Unit weight | Quantity | Contribution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Coffee A | 500 g | 2 | 1 kg |
| Coffee B | 1 kg | 3 | 3 kg |
| Total | | | 4 kg |

Supported units are `g`, `kg`, `oz` and `lb`. Mixed units convert automatically. A weight equal to the threshold satisfies it.

## Product and customer scope

A selected-product or collection rule sums only matching items. A cart without those items is not required to buy them.

Tag conditions can match customers with or without specified tags. Every matching weight rule is checked, so avoid conflicting overlapping ranges.

<Warning>Packaging is not included. The rule does not set shipping charges, split packages or select carriers.</Warning>

Try [a 20 kg merchandise cap](/en/scenarios/shipping-weight).

## Set a minimum or weight range

Fill at least one threshold. For a range, the maximum must not be below the minimum. Choose the unit before entering values. For customer groups, use matching and non-matching tag conditions to avoid incompatible overlapping ranges. Follow the [launch checklist](/en/getting-started/launch-checklist).
