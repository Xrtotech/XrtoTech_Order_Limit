## Chinese-language support

You can communicate in Chinese about using Order Limit. Describe the purchase condition you need, such as “VIP customers can buy up to 4 units per order,” then explain your settings and actual results so your requirements are clear.

## Documentation in three languages

Read the guides in Chinese, Japanese or English. All three cover the same features and scenarios. Settings follow the actual Chinese app labels, and translations retain a Chinese label reference. See [find the settings in the app](/en/getting-started/how-it-works#find-the-settings-in-the-app).

## Prepare an issue report

1. Describe the expected rule: applicable products, customer conditions and minimum or maximum values.
2. Record the actual result: units, merchandise amount or weight, and whether checkout was allowed.
3. Include relevant rule settings, feature switches and message screenshots. For customer rules, describe sign-in status and test tags.
4. List steps to reproduce the issue and hide customer names, emails, addresses and other personal information.

Start with [troubleshooting](/en/troubleshooting/rules-not-working), then include these details when reporting the issue.
