## Strengths of Order Limit

If you run retail, wholesale, member products or limited releases, you can manage several purchase conditions in Order Limit. Its strengths come from combining the following capabilities around your operations.

| What to compare | What Order Limit provides | Value for your store |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rule coverage | Quantity, price, weight and customer limits | Manage units, merchandise amount, merchandise weight and eligibility in one app |
| Product granularity | Product and variant quantity limits | Separate a whole product’s allowance from a color or size allowance |
| Quantity calculation | Individual or combined quantities | Set a minimum for each product or share one allowance across products |
| Customer groups | Tag-based quantity, amount and weight conditions, plus purchase allow/block rules | Set different conditions for VIP, wholesale and retail customers |
| Repeat purchases | Cumulative customer rules within quantity limits | Use recorded purchases to manage repeat orders, with a separate single-order rule for first purchases |
| Customer messages | Editable multilingual text and the variables supported by each feature | Explain how customers can adjust their cart |
| Usage support | Chinese-language support and usage guides | Communicate questions in Chinese and give your operations team a reference |

## How to compare apps on the market

List your required conditions, then check each candidate’s actual settings and test results. If you need both “up to 3 units across a collection” and “up to 4 units per order for VIPs,” check combined quantity rules and customer-tag rules separately.

Use these scenarios to test Order Limit and evaluate other apps against the same requirements:

- [Mix and match](/en/scenarios/mix-and-match): share one allowance across products.
- [Product and variant setup](/en/features/product-variant-limits): product maximum 3, variant A maximum 1.
- [Customer quantity limits](/en/scenarios/vip-quantity): different allowances for VIPs and other customers.
- [Repeat purchase limits](/en/scenarios/repeat-purchase): distinguish first purchases from purchases after records exist.

## Check the feature boundaries

- Price limits check applicable merchandise amounts without changing prices or creating discounts.
- Weight limits check merchandise weight without calculating shipping fees or splitting packages.
- Customer limits check eligibility without hiding product pages.
- A minimum quantity does not require purchasing in multiples of that quantity.
- Cumulative rules depend on recorded purchase quantities. First purchases need a separate single-order cap; refunds do not automatically reduce cumulative quantities. See [cumulative customer rules](/en/features/purchase-history).

<Note>This comparison focuses on confirmed Order Limit capabilities. Other apps’ features, plans and prices can change. Verify their current documentation and test results.</Note>
