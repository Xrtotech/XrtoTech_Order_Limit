When using Order Limit, distinguish single-cart limits, buying eligibility and recorded purchase history. These answers clarify commonly confused settings.

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Is a per-order limit a lifetime customer limit?">
No. Ordinary quantity rules check the current cart. For repeat purchases, use [cumulative rules](/en/features/purchase-history) and add a separate first-order quantity cap.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can I require multiples of 6?">
The current editor has minimum, maximum and calculation settings, but no step or multiple setting. A minimum of 6 allows 7.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Why can a customer add 1 unit when the minimum is 6?">
Quantity minimums check at checkout so customers can build the cart incrementally.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Do different colors count together?">
Product settings and individual quantity rules combine variants of the same product. Variant settings check separately. Use combined calculation to share an allowance across different products.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Must customers match every listed tag?">
No. Any one listed tag is a match. An allowlist lets customers buy when at least one required tag matches.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Will customer restrictions hide products or prices?">
No. They check buying eligibility, not product visibility or page access.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Do weight rules set shipping fees or split packages?">
No. They only check applicable merchandise weight. Configure packaging, shipping charges and fulfillment separately.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Do amount thresholds use compare-at prices?">
No. They use current applicable cart line amounts, with thresholds entered in the shop’s base currency. See [price calculation](/en/features/price).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can I reset customer allowances daily or monthly?">
The current cumulative rule has no time window or scheduled reset. Do not use it to promise daily or monthly allowances.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Are delivery, payment method or bundle restrictions available?">
This manual documents the verified quantity, price, weight and customer features. It does not present delivery methods, payment methods, page access or bundle rules as existing app features.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Why check switches after saving a rule?">
Saving, active status, category switches and validation are separate checks. Quantity rules also need **购物车与结账校验**. Follow the complete [quickstart](/en/quickstart).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="How do I pause a rule temporarily?">
Choose **草稿** and click **保存为草稿**. Restore with **已启用** and save. Product and variant table rows use **限制状态**. See [pause and restore](/en/getting-started/launch-checklist).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Do all customers need a VIP tag just to sign in?">
No. Leave customer tags empty and enable **需要客户账户登录**. Follow [require sign-in](/en/scenarios/login-required).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Does a collection minimum force customers to buy it?">
No. A scoped rule checks only when the cart contains matching items. Also test a cart with unrelated products only.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Does changing the help center language translate store messages?">
No. Configure each category's customer messages separately, then test storefront or checkout language. See [messages and languages](/en/settings/messages).
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

For configuration issues, follow [troubleshooting](/en/troubleshooting/rules-not-working).

Unsure which limit fits? Start with [About Order Limit](/en/index), then use the [launch checklist](/en/getting-started/launch-checklist).
