Order Limit has different entries for different goals. Write down <strong>who can buy, which products are covered and what threshold each purchase must meet</strong>. Start with that goal rather than every available setting.

## Match your goal to a recipe

| Goal | Rule | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Up to 2 units including different colors of one product | Individual product quantity | [Limited release](/en/scenarios/limited-release) |
| Up to 3 units across several products | Combined quantity | [Mix and match](/en/scenarios/mix-and-match) |
| At least 6 units of each product | Individual minimum | [Wholesale minimum](/en/scenarios/wholesale-minimum) |
| Blue variant maximum 1, whole product maximum 3 | Product and variant limits | [Product and variant setup](/en/getting-started/product-variant-limits) |
| VIPs get 4 units, others get 2 | Customer-tag quantity | [VIP allowance](/en/scenarios/vip-quantity) |
| Merchandise amount at least 100 | Price rule | [Minimum amount](/en/scenarios/minimum-order-value) |
| Wholesale minimum 500, others 100 | Customer-tag price | [Group minimums](/en/scenarios/customer-pricing) |
| Merchandise weight no more than 20 kg | Weight rule | [Weight cap](/en/scenarios/shipping-weight) |
| Only VIPs can buy a collection | Customer-tag allowlist | [Member exclusives](/en/scenarios/members-only) |
| All buyers must sign in, with no tag condition | Sign-in-only customer rule | [Require sign-in](/en/scenarios/login-required) |
| A tagged group cannot buy promotional items | Customer-tag blocklist | [Block customers](/en/scenarios/block-customers) |
| Prevent repeat sample purchases for one account | Single-order plus cumulative rule | [Repeat samples](/en/scenarios/repeat-purchase) |

## Define four settings first

1. **Product scope**: all products, a fixed list or a collection that changes over time?
2. **Customers**: everyone, tagged customers, untagged customers or signed-in customers?
3. **Calculation**: each product separately or a shared threshold across matching items?
4. **Timing**: cart interactions and checkout, or checkout only? Quantity minimums always wait until checkout.

For “up to 3 across a collection,” combined calculation matters. Individual calculation gives each product its own allowance of 3.

## Keep similar requests separate

- A cap of 2 per order is different from a recorded lifetime allowance.
- A minimum of 6 allows 7; it does not enforce multiples of 6.
- Price rules do not change prices, add discounts or create free shipping.
- Purchase eligibility does not hide product pages, prices or collections.
- Weight checks do not split parcels, calculate shipping or select carriers.
