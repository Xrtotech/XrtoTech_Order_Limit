Use the product table in Order Limit for independent product or variant thresholds. This example caps all variants of a product at 3 units, while its variant B variant has a separate cap of 1.

## Before you begin

Prepare variants A and B of the same purchasable product. Review [rule priority](/en/settings/rule-priority): an overlapping individual quantity rule can relax the product cap.

## Set both levels

<Steps>
<Step title="Find the product">
Open **Apps** → **订单限制** in Shopify. Choose **按产品设置数量限制** on the app home page. Search for the product under **搜索产品**.
</Step>
<Step title="Set the whole-product cap">
On the product row, set **限制状态** to **已开启**, leave **最小值** blank and enter `3` under **最大值**. This counts all variants of the product together.
</Step>
<Step title="Set the variant B-variant cap">
Expand the product variants. On the variant B row, select **已开启**, leave the minimum blank and set the maximum to `1`. Click **保存** at the top and wait for saving to finish.
</Step>
<Step title="Enable the feature and validation">
Return to **数量限制**, turn on **开启产品数量限制**, and choose **购物车互动时和结账时**. Confirm **数量规则开关** is enabled, open the **数量规则** switch until it shows **已开启**.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Test four carts

| Cart | Result for this example | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| variant A × 3 | Allowed | Product total is 3 |
| variant B × 1 + variant A × 2 | Allowed | Total 3, variant B within 1 |
| variant B × 2 | Blocked | variant B exceeds 1 |
| variant B × 1 + variant A × 3 | Blocked | Product total exceeds 3 |

Other active rules can still block a cart that passes these settings.

## Edit or pause

Change the row values and click **保存**. To pause one row, set **限制状态** to **未开启** and save. Disabling a product row does not disable its active variant rows; check each separately.

Turning off **开启产品数量限制** pauses these product and variant settings. It is separate from **数量规则**.

## If results differ

If variant B × 2 passes, check that you edited the variant B variant row and saved. If the whole product exceeds 3, check product status, the feature switch and overlapping individual rules. Product and individual rule limits combine using relaxed thresholds.

For a shared allowance across products, use [combined quantity](/en/scenarios/mix-and-match). Complete the [launch checklist](/en/getting-started/launch-checklist). The bold Chinese labels above match the current app.
